## Description

HDMI Captain turns your Mac or iPad into a monitor for anything with an HDMI output — game consoles, cameras, computers and media players — using an inexpensive USB HDMI capture card.

ALWAYS ON
• The picture never drops when you switch windows, Spaces or desktops on the Mac.
• Cards are found automatically, reconnect on their own and remember their settings.
• Clear screens for no signal, HDCP-protected sources and missing permissions tell you exactly what to do.

LOW LATENCY, SHOWN NOT CLAIMED
• Metal rendering straight from the card with no extra buffering.
• Built-in diagnostics show frame rate, dropped frames and measured latency.

PICTURE AND SOUND, YOUR WAY
• Fit, fill, stretch or pixel-perfect integer scaling; sharp or smooth filtering.
• Picture looks, brightness, contrast, saturation, gamma and sharpness; .cube LUTs.
• HDMI sound with volume, audio delay to fix lip-sync, and left/right or mono fixes.
• Guides, safe areas, test patterns and scopes for creators.
• Console profiles remember the best settings for each source.

MADE FOR MAC AND IPAD
• Mac: floating window, size presets, full screen, menu bar control, keyboard shortcuts, a window per card and a multi-view grid.
• iPad: full-screen viewing with touch controls, a clean picture on an external display, and instant resume when you come back to the app.
• A control bar appears only when you need it and never shows up in screenshots or recordings.

FREE VIEWER, ONE-TIME PRO
The viewer, screenshots and every picture setting are free. HDMI Captain Pro is a single purchase — no subscription — and adds:
• Recording: save full-quality HEVC or H.264 video with the HDMI sound.
• Save Recent: missed a great moment? Save the last 15 seconds to 60 minutes after it happened.

PRIVATE
No accounts, no tracking, no data collection. Video and sound stay on your device.

Requirements: a UVC (driver-free) USB HDMI capture card. On iPad, an iPad with USB-C running iPadOS 17 or later. Sources that use HDCP (for example streaming apps) can't be captured by any capture card.

## App Review notes

Testing needs a USB UVC HDMI capture card (for example UGREEN 1080p60 cards) and any HDMI source. Without one, the app shows its "Connect a capture card" screen. To exercise the viewer without hardware on the Mac: Video › Test Pattern shows built-in test patterns, and Video › Show Diagnostics shows the stats overlay.
The app asks for camera and microphone access because the system presents capture cards as a camera and a microphone. Nothing is transmitted; there are no accounts.
In-app purchase: one non-consumable "HDMI Captain Pro" unlocks Recording and Save Recent. Restore Purchase is in the upgrade sheet and Settings › Pro.
Live streaming and virtual camera code exists but is hidden and not offered in this version.
