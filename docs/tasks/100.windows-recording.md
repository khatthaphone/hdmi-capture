## Steps

1. `Recorder`: MF Sink Writer, hardware encoder MFT chosen with `MFTEnumEx(HARDWARE)` on the render adapter (NVENC/AMF/QSV), software H.264 fallback, AAC from the audio pipeline, fragmented MP4 to `Videos\HDMI Captain`. Feed it from the pass-1 texture (D3D11 surface samples, no CPU readback where the MFT accepts D3D input).
2. `RollingRecorder`: encoded sample deque trimmed at IDR frames, forced keyframe every 2 s, passthrough remux on save (15/30/60 s options); `-qaAction recent15`, `-qaAction record3s`.
3. A/V sync from capture timestamps (QPC); recording survives unplug (the file is closed cleanly and playable).
4. Gate both through `ProGate` (099).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A 10-min 1080p60 recording plays in the Windows Media Player and VLC with A/V in sync (check the clap at start and end)
- [ ] Save Recent saves the last 15 s within 1 s of the click, with no re-encode
- [ ] Recordings contain no UI

## Log

- 2026-10-22 — created (Windows plan from `docs/research/windows.md`)
- 2026-10-22 — Media layer checkpoint (`src/HDMICaptain.Media/Recording/`). Our own encoder MFTs feed a passthrough Sink Writer: `Nv12Converter` turns the pass-1 RGBA texture into BT.709 limited NV12 on the GPU, in a pool of 8 textures (render thread, `D3DRenderer.SourceRendered`, raised after present). `VideoEncoder` uses the render adapter's hardware MFT (matched by vendor), async, bound to the render device through `MFDXGIDeviceManager`, fed D3D11 surface samples with no readback. It falls back to the inbox software H.264 MFT through a staging copy (WARP, or no hardware encoder). No B-frames, GOP = keyframe interval, and a forced keyframe every 2 s. `AudioEncoder` is the inbox AAC MFT (192 kbps, PCM16 in) fed by the new `WasapiAudioPipeline.CapturedAudio` tap (QPC from `GetBuffer`). Both tracks use QPC minus the first frame's time; audio is count-based and jumps forward on gaps over 40 ms. `Recorder` writes fragmented MP4 (`MFTranscodeContainerType_FMPEG4`, with 'moof' boxes checked in a test). `RollingRecorder` keeps encoded samples in memory and trims whole GOPs. `SaveAsync` remuxes from the keyframe at or before the cut, with no re-encode. On this PC the RTX 5070 Ti picks **NVIDIA H.264 Encoder MFT** with D3D11 input. Tests (WARP software and RTX hardware, SMPTE bars + 1 kHz tone, decoded bar colours checked within ±6/±8): 3 s recordings come back at 1920×1080, 90/180 frames, 3.00 s video, AAC 48 kHz stereo 3.01 s. Save Recent of 15 s out of 20 s gives 16.0 s (it starts at a keyframe) in **28–39 ms**, with the same sample count and byte count ±16 bytes as the buffer. Real card (`HDMICaptain.Inventory --match UGREEN --record 15`, DEBUG; the card was shared with the running app, which took retries with `MF_E_HW_MFT_FAILED_START_STREAMING`): 865 frames at 60 fps, 5 dropped at start while NVENC came up, video 14.515 s / audio 14.507 s, stop (drain + finalize) 8 ms. Save Recent of 7 s gave 8.47 s in 29 ms.
