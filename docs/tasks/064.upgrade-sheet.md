## Objective

The upgrade sheet is the one place that earns money: make it clear and honest. Show what Pro adds with visuals, the one-time price, restore, and no "coming soon" clutter for features that aren't shipping.

## Relevant files / modules

- `Apps/Shared/Store/UpgradeView.swift` — `ProFeature.available`, `.hidden`, `UpgradeView`
- `Apps/Shared/Store/EntitlementStore.swift` — purchase and restore states
- `docs/spec.md` §10 — business model (free viewer + one-time Pro)

## Steps

1. Decide with the user whether unbuilt features (Virtual Camera) are hidden like streaming (046) or kept as "coming soon", and log it.
2. Redesign: header with the app icon (060), 2–3 feature rows with small illustrations (record, Save Recent), price button, Restore, and a "The viewer stays free" line. Make it work in an iPad sheet and a Mac sheet.
3. Purchased state: thank-you plus feature list; pending (Ask to Buy) and failed states keep their wording from 063.
