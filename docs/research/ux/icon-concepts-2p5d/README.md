# HDMI Capture — 2.5D icon exploration

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool on 2026-10-21. These are concept assets only; the shipping app icon was not changed.

## Directions

- **A — Folded Monitor:** A dark graphite monitor with a recessed HDMI socket and one orange capture pixel. Very literal and immediately readable, but closer to hardware illustration than a distinctive brand mark.
- **B — Port Window:** A cream monitor/display with a large HDMI-shaped screen recess and three descending pixels. Clear concept with restrained materials, though the monitor stand makes it conventional.
- **C — Pixel Console:** A cream display with an amber HDMI socket and four raised capture pixels on forest green. Friendly and tactile, but busier and more playful.
- **D — Sculpted Signal:** A continuous ivory screen frame folds into an HDMI socket on oxblood, with one gold pixel. The strongest brand direction: memorable silhouette, minimal palette, and shallow dimensionality.

## Shared art direction

Premium 2.5D app icon; shallow extrusion; matte ceramic or soft-touch polymer; soft studio lighting; compact shadows; HDMI port plus display and pixel cues; no blue or cyan; no cable, text, letters, official HDMI logo, neon, glossy gaming effects, or photorealistic circuitry.

## Recommended refinement

Advance **D** while simplifying the small socket contacts into three larger square pixel/contact forms. Reduce surface grain, retain the oxblood–ivory–muted-gold palette, and keep the straight-on silhouette so it remains legible at 32 px.
