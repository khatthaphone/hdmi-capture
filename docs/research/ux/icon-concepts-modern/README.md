## Naming exploration

### Working decision

**HDMI Capture** — selected as the plain working product name. The name prioritizes immediate comprehension and search intent over a proprietary brand metaphor. Recommended storefront presentation: **HDMI Capture** with the subtitle **Low-Latency HDMI Monitor**.

The exact name did not surface as an obvious current Apple-platform app title in a preliminary web scan, although nearby titles such as “UVC Capture — HDMI Monitor” exist. “HDMI” is a registered trademark, and HDMI Licensing Administrator publishes usage rules specifically covering mobile/PC software. Treat this as a working name until the current trademark guidelines and storefront availability are reviewed for launch.

### Recommended shortlist

1. **SignalPane** — the incoming signal becomes a clean viewing pane. Modern, native-feeling, and broad enough for recording and streaming later.
2. **Portglass** — an ownable compound: an HDMI port turned into a window. Best paired with concept C.
3. **FrameRelay** — professional and technical; suggests a low-latency path from source to display.
4. **Input One** — minimal and utility-like, though less distinctive and harder to own.
5. **Captain View** — a softer evolution that preserves recognition while dropping the ornate nautical treatment.

Suggested App Store presentation: **SignalPane — HDMI Monitor** or **Portglass — HDMI Monitor & Recorder**. The short brand can stay clean while the subtitle explains the product.

### Names excluded after a quick web conflict scan

FramePilot, SignalDeck, InputScope, PortFrame, FrameDock, ViewRelay, SourceFlow, SignalHelm, SignalGlass, SignalBay, FrameTap, SignalTap, and Frameward already have active products, apps, or closely related software uses.

This is a preliminary product-name scan, not trademark clearance. Before changing bundle metadata or publishing, check Apple storefronts in target countries, domains/social handles, and the relevant trademark registers.
