# Codex design review — Mac screens (2026-10-21)

Generated by `codex exec` (codex-cli 0.153) with the screenshots in `shots/mac-*.png` attached. Verbatim answer:

## Overall assessment

HDMI Captain’s core concept is strong: the live picture stays dominant, the chrome is restrained, and the app generally feels native. The main quality gaps are state communication, overlay legibility, semantic feedback, and trust around Pro.

I reviewed the screenshots and relevant implementation in [ContentView.swift](/Users/khatthaphone/Projects/hdmi-captain/Apps/Shared/ContentView.swift), [ControlBar.swift](/Users/khatthaphone/Projects/hdmi-captain/Apps/Shared/ControlBar.swift), [UpgradeView.swift](/Users/khatthaphone/Projects/hdmi-captain/Apps/Shared/Store/UpgradeView.swift), and [SettingsView.swift](/Users/khatthaphone/Projects/hdmi-captain/Apps/macOS/Sources/SettingsView.swift). The image labeled `settings-input` appears to duplicate the Pro tab, so Input-specific findings below also use the source.

The priorities reflect Apple’s current guidance: explain protected-resource access before requesting it, use progress indicators only for ongoing work, maintain contrast, and provide at least 44×44-point touch targets on iPad. [Privacy HIG](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/privacy/), [Progress indicators](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/progress-indicators), [Accessibility](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/accessibility), [UI design tips](https://developer.apple.com/design/tips/).

### P1 — Address before release

| Screen | Problem | Why it matters | Concrete fix | Severity |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| First launch | Camera and microphone prompts are initiated immediately, before the product explains why a capture card needs those permissions. | “Camera” and “microphone” sound alarming for an HDMI viewer. This is the first trust test. | Show one pre-permission page: `cable.connector.horizontal`, title **“Connect your capture card”**, text **“macOS classifies USB capture cards as cameras and microphones. HDMI Captain uses access only for devices you select.”** One **Continue** button, then the system camera prompt. | P1 |
| First launch / audio | Microphone access is requested after camera access, but denial has no visible state; video silently works without sound. | A second unexplained prompt feels invasive, and silent output looks like a defect. | Request audio only when a selected card exposes audio. If denied, keep video live and show a nonblocking **“Audio access is off”** banner with **Open Microphone Settings**. Purpose string: **“HDMI Captain uses microphone access to play audio from the selected capture device.”** | P1 |
| Recovering / no signal / waiting | Hardware failure, missing HDMI signal, and physical unplugging are not cleanly separated. “No signal — Reconnecting automatically…” implies software reconnection can fix source-side HDMI loss. | Incorrect recovery language destroys confidence and sends users toward the wrong fix. | Use three distinct states: **No HDMI signal** + `rectangle.slash`; **Reconnecting to UGREEN-25854…** + `arrow.clockwise` and spinner; **UGREEN-25854 disconnected** + `cable.connector.slash`. Never reconnect-loop solely because the HDMI source is absent. | P1 |
| Live control bar | The translucent material becomes washed out over white/cyan test bars; text, sliders, and separators lose contrast. | Video is an uncontrolled background. HIG contrast cannot depend on the incoming picture. | Use a stable dark foundation: black at roughly 72–78% opacity, 1-point white stroke at 16–18%, and current shadow. Under Increase Contrast, make it about 88% opaque. Do not let material choose the bar’s luminance. | P1 |
| All toasts | Every toast receives `checkmark.circle.fill`, including **“Turn off the test pattern to record”**, **“Saving…”**, and recording failures. | A success mark attached to an error is a direct semantic contradiction; color/icon semantics become meaningless. | Replace the string-only model with `success`, `progress`, `warning`, and `error`. Use `checkmark.circle.fill`, spinner, `exclamationmark.triangle.fill`, and `xmark.octagon.fill`. Reserve the checkmark strictly for completed actions. | P1 |
| Pro sheet / Pro Settings | Virtual camera is sold as “Included in Pro” while labeled “Coming soon”; Settings omits even that qualifier. | A paid sheet must describe what the purchase delivers now. “Coming soon” inside the purchase value proposition is commercially ambiguous. | Remove Virtual Camera from both paid-feature lists until it ships. If roadmap communication is essential, put it outside the purchase sheet under **Planned**, with no implication that availability or timing is guaranteed. | P1 |
| Input, Picture, Sound Settings | `.disabled(viewer.session == nil)` disables the entire card form, including the device picker that could help establish a session. | The recovery path can become functionally and visually dead. Disabled controls also provide no explanation. | Keep **Capture card** enabled whenever devices exist. Disable only card-dependent fields such as Format and Picture. Above them show `cable.connector.slash` and **“Connect or select a capture card to edit these settings.”** | P1 |
| iPad control bar | Shared bar controls have 28-point minimum dimensions. | Suitable for compact Mac controls, but below Apple’s 44×44-point touch recommendation for iPad. | Use platform metrics: 28–32-point items on Mac; minimum 44×44 on iPad, with 8-point inter-item spacing. In minimal iPad layout keep Input, Record, Save Recent, and More as four unambiguous targets. | P1 |

### P2 — Strong quality improvements

| Screen | Problem | Why it matters | Concrete fix | Severity |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| All full-screen statuses | The centered groups use inconsistent combinations of no icon, spinner, small symbol, and illustration. Titles are visually close to body copy. | Repeated system states should feel like one designed family and be identifiable at a glance. | Standardize on: 40-point symbol/indicator, 20–22-point semibold title, 13–14-point secondary copy, 420-point maximum text width, 8 points title-to-copy, 16 points before actions. | P2 |
| Scanning / connecting / waiting | Spinners are used or expected even when the app is actually waiting for the user to connect hardware. | Apple defines progress indicators as transient indicators of ongoing work. A user-dependent wait is an empty state, not progress. | Use an illustration only for the long-lived **connect a card** state: source → HDMI card → USB-C device. Use a spinner only during actual connection and automatic recovery. Use static SF Symbols for permission, unplugged, and failure states. | P2 |
| Scanning | **“Scanning for hardware”** sounds perpetual and gives no useful next step. | After a second or two, users will wonder whether the app supports their device. | After a brief discovery pass, change to **“No capture card found”** and **“Connect a UVC-compatible HDMI capture card. It will appear automatically.”** Add **Connection Help…**; on Mac optionally offer **Try a built-in camera**. | P2 |
| Connecting | The screen can remain on a terse **“Connecting to…”** message without escalation. | Long indeterminate waits look frozen. | Show spinner + **“Connecting to UGREEN-25854…”**. After roughly 8 seconds add **“This is taking longer than expected.”** with **Reconnect** and **Connection Help…**. Label the spinner for VoiceOver. | P2 |
| Permission denied | **“Camera access is off”** is understandable, but the hierarchy and generic **Try Again** action are weak. | Recovery should name the exact setting and expected result. | Title **“Camera access required”**; body **“Allow HDMI Captain to use capture cards in System Settings, then return here.”** Primary **Open Camera Settings**, secondary **Check Again**. Use `video.slash`, 40 points. | P2 |
| Waiting / unplugged | **“The picture comes back as soon as it’s plugged in”** is friendly but indirect. | The user needs confirmation that the exact remembered card is being watched. | Use **“UGREEN-25854 disconnected”** and **“Reconnect it to resume automatically.”** Keep `cable.connector.slash`; add **Use Another Card…** for pinned windows. | P2 |
| Failed | **“Can’t show video”** plus **Try Again** hides whether the problem is the device, format, or renderer. | Generic errors make the app feel brittle and provide poor recovery. | Prefer **“UGREEN-25854 stopped responding”** with the plain-language reason. Primary **Reconnect**, secondary **Details…** or **Troubleshooting…**. Keep technical AVFoundation/Metal text behind Details. | P2 |
| No signal | The likely HDCP/cable explanation is either absent or potentially becomes a long paragraph over the picture. | Signal loss needs a concise immediate action and optional deeper help. | Title **“No HDMI signal”**; body **“Check the source power and HDMI cable.”** Add **Troubleshoot Signal…**, covering HDCP and console-specific steps. Use `rectangle.slash`; do not show a spinner. | P2 |
| Control bar | The grouping is visually subtle, and `gobackward` alone makes Save Recent resemble undo/history. | Capture actions should scan as a coherent group. | Arrange `[Input | Audio]  [Screenshot | Record | Save Recent]  [View | Full Screen]`, with 8-point group gaps and 1-point dividers. Use `clock.arrow.circlepath` for Save Recent; keep `camera` and `record.circle` for the other capture actions. | P2 |
| Control bar / test pattern | The bar says **No Input** while a test pattern fills the viewer; Record then produces an error toast. | The visible source and selected-source label disagree. | When a test pattern is active, label the source chip **Test Pattern** with `rectangle.split.3x1`. Disable Record with help **“Switch to live input to record”**, or provide a toast action **Show Live Input**. | P2 |
| Recording | Recording state is clearest only while the bar is visible; its separate indicator can be disabled. | Hidden chrome makes accidental long recordings and disk use easier. State must not rely on red alone. | Always retain a small top-right red dot plus elapsed timer while recording. Rename the preference **Show recording timer** and let it control the timer’s prominence, not whether all recording indication disappears. Record button becomes **Stop · 00:12** with a square symbol. | P2 |
| Compact control bar | Labels disappear broadly in compact mode, making less familiar actions dependent on tooltips that iPad does not have. | Screenshot, Save Recent, and input/profile concepts are not all universally inferred from icons. | Preserve text for the primary current action—**Record** or elapsed time—and accessible names for every icon. On iPad, show a short label under or beside Save Recent when space permits; never rely on hover help. | P2 |
| Toast placement | Toast padding assumes a 66-point bar and doesn’t account for compact/minimal layouts, stats, small windows, or multiple indicators. | Overlays can collide or feel arbitrarily positioned. | Measure the actual control-bar height and place feedback 12 points above it. Establish fixed overlay zones: status top-center, diagnostics top-left, recording top-right, transient feedback bottom-center. | P2 |
| Action-required feedback | Errors disappear after 2.5 seconds and are noninteractive because toasts ignore hit testing. | Users may miss the cause and cannot recover directly. | Keep success feedback for 2.5–3 seconds; warnings/errors for 5–6 seconds. For recoverable failures use an actionable banner: **“Recording unavailable while Test Pattern is on” · Show Live Input**. | P2 |
| Pro sheet | **“One purchase, yours forever. The viewer stays free.”** is good, but “Unlock for $9.99” is less standard and “no subscription” is only implied. | Purchase terms should be immediately scannable and unambiguous. | Subtitle **“One-time purchase. No subscription.”** Then **“The live viewer remains free.”** CTA **“Buy Pro — $9.99”** using localized StoreKit price. If entered through Record, title the sheet **“Record with HDMI Captain Pro”**. | P2 |
| Pro sheet | Restore Purchases competes visually with the purchase decision; feature descriptions mix benefits with codec terminology. | The primary decision should be clear without hiding required restoration. | Keep Restore Purchases as a quiet secondary button/link. Use benefit-first copy: **Recording — Save full-quality video and HDMI audio.** **Save Recent — Save what just happened, from 15 seconds to 60 minutes.** | P2 |
| Settings window | Tabs have different fixed widths/heights; Pro text is visibly clipped at the bottom and page changes can resize the window. | Window jumping and clipped help text make the Settings shell feel unfinished. | Give all tabs a common approximately 560×620-point content area with vertical scrolling. Keep the six-tab toolbar stationary and preserve the user-resized window. | P2 |
| Picture Settings | A completely pale, disabled form looks broken when no card is active. | Disabled controls communicate restriction, not absence of context. | Replace the form with `ContentUnavailableView`: **“No active capture card”**, `cable.connector.slash`, **“Connect a card or bring a viewer window forward.”** Keep LUT removal and global options available where possible. | P2 |
| Settings wording | Several labels are technical or inconsistent across Mac and iPad. | Consistent terminology reduces relearning across menu, bar, and Settings. | Use **Scale**; **Image filtering: Smooth / Pixel-sharp**; **Keep window on top**; **Hide window controls**; **Show viewer controls near the bottom edge**; **Audio output**; **Play audio from the frontmost viewer**. Use the same capitalization everywhere. | P2 |
| Profiles | **“Apply it to the card in the active window”** is ambiguous because Settings is the active window. Disabled Apply buttons do not identify the target. | Users cannot confidently predict which viewer will change. | Footer: **“Profiles apply to the most recently used HDMI Captain viewer.”** When available, label the button **Apply to UGREEN-25854**. Otherwise show **“Open or select a viewer to apply profiles.”** | P2 |
| Pro Settings | Free users can alter parts of Save Recent configuration even though the feature is locked, and the storage footer contains navigation/shortcut instructions. | Lock state and configuration state are mixed; the footer is hard to scan and currently clips. | Lock the entire Save Recent configuration with `lock.fill` and **Requires Pro**, while keeping **Upgrade to Pro…** available. Footer: **“Temporarily uses about 1.4 GB at the current input format. Footage is saved only when you choose Save Recent.”** | P2 |
| Status/accessibility | State changes and disappearing feedback appear to rely primarily on visual observation. | VoiceOver users may never learn that connection, recovery, recording, or save state changed. | Post polite accessibility announcements for connecting/saved states and assertive ones for failures. Give indicators full labels, avoid announcing the recording timer every second, and replace decorative animations under Reduce Motion. | P2 |
| iPad first use / paused | There is no upfront cable guidance, USB-C compatibility note, or explanation of OS-driven multitasking pauses. | Hardware topology and iPad camera restrictions are unfamiliar even to experienced users. | Add a concise connection diagram and **“Requires an iPad with USB-C and a UVC capture card.”** On the first multitasking pause, show one TipKit tip explaining how to resume in full screen or a supported Stage Manager layout. | P2 |
| App icon | No finished identity is visible; a literal captain, ship wheel, camera, or SMPTE bars would be generic or too detailed at 16 px. | The icon is the first and most repeated quality signal across Dock, Finder, Settings, and iPad Home Screen. | Use a dark navy layered tile with a single bold HDMI connector/cable forming a subtle **C**, plus one electric-blue signal highlight. No text or test bars. Build 1024×1024 background/foreground layers in Icon Composer; supply default, dark, clear, and tinted appearances and verify at 16 px. [App icon HIG](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/app-icons) | P2 |

### P3 — Refinement

| Screen | Problem | Why it matters | Concrete fix | Severity |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Screenshot/save toast | **“Saved HDMI Captain 2026-10-21 at 03.10.59.png”** is long and exposes implementation-style naming. | Success feedback should confirm the action, not make users parse a filename. | Use **“Screenshot saved”** with optional **Show in Finder**. Put the filename in the tooltip/action destination. Recording: **“Recording saved · 12:43”**. | P3 |
| Stats overlay | The source renders dense diagnostic prose—requested/actual mode, in/out fps, dropped frames, two latency measurements, and audio—in 11-point type. | It reads like a developer HUD and competes with the content. | Rename the option **Show Diagnostics**. Default to two lines: `1920×1080 · 59.94 fps · 0 dropped` and `≈28 ms latency · Stereo`; expose detailed values through a disclosure/popover. Use black at about 78% opacity. | P3 |
| Sound Settings | The level meter lacks visible L/R labels and an accessible numeric value; “follows the focused window” is hard to predict. | Audio routing mistakes are difficult to diagnose visually or with VoiceOver. | Add **L** and **R**, peak-hold ticks, and accessibility value such as **“Left minus 18 decibels.”** Rename the toggle **“Play audio from the frontmost viewer”** with a short explanatory footer. | P3 |
| Picture Settings | Adjustment sliders show no values, making looks difficult to reproduce or compare. | Professional video tools benefit from precision even when defaults remain simple. | Show trailing values: Brightness `+0.00`, Contrast `1.00×`, Saturation `100%`, Gamma `1.00`, Sharpness `0%`. Double-clicking a slider should restore its default; keep **Reset Look** for the whole group. | P3 |
| Window Settings | Guides and viewer behavior are presented as long undifferentiated switch lists. | Functional, but visually flat and harder to scan. | Add section labels **Viewer**, **App Presence**, and **Guides**. Place guide opacity directly beneath the guide toggles and disable it only when no guide or mask is active. | P3 |

## Five changes with the greatest perceived-quality impact

1. Build one coherent first-run and status system that clearly separates permission, no card, no HDMI signal, reconnecting, and failure.
2. Give the control bar a reliably dark, high-contrast surface and platform-correct Mac/iPad sizing.
3. Replace string-only toasts with semantic, actionable feedback and a persistent recording state.
4. Make the Pro offer completely honest: only shipping features, “one-time purchase/no subscription,” and benefit-first copy.
5. Finish the product shell: stable Settings geometry, recoverable no-session states, and a distinctive layered HDMI-connector icon.
